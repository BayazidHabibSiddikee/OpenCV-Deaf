Sign Language Reference Card

Complete guide to all sign gestures with meanings - ASL, BSL, ISL

👋
Hello
Wave hello to someone
ASL BSL ISL
Common greeting - move hand side to side or wave
👍
Yes
Nod head to indicate agreement
ASL BSL ISL
Thumbs up gesture - use in affirmation
✊
No
Shake head to indicate disagreement
ASL BSL ISL
Fist gesture - move hand side to side
✌
Peace
Peace sign - victory or peace
ASL BSL ISL
Index and middle finger raised - use carefully around strangers
⌛
One
Count to one or point to something
ASL BSL ISL
Index finger only raised - pointing
👌
OK
All is good or agreement
ASL BSL ISL
Thumb and index form a circle - OK / approved
✌
Three
Count to three or peace
ASL BSL ISL
Index, middle, ring finger raised
👋
Five
Count to five or open palm
ASL BSL ISL
All fingers extended - open palm
❤️
Love
Express affection or love
ASL BSL ISL
Heart shape with hands or gesture
🙏
Thank You
Express gratitude
ASL BSL ISL
Fingers near chin, move forward and down
🙏
Please
Request or ask politely
ASL BSL ISL
Flat hand on chest, move in circular motion
😔
Sorry
Express apology
ASL BSL ISL
Fist or flat hand over chest, move down
🆘
Help
Request assistance
ASL BSL ISL
Horizontal fist, other hand clasps thumb side
🤟
I Love You
Express love or affection
ASL BSL ISL
Thumb, index, pinky extended - ILY gesture
✋
Stop
Halt or cease action
ASL BSL ISL
Open palm, extend arm and hold still
🕐
Wait
Ask someone to wait or pause
ASL BSL ISL
Hand in wait position or hold up
📚
Learn
Study or acquire knowledge
ASL BSL ISL
Hold book to chest with both hands
👩‍♀️
Teacher
Instructor or educator
ASL BSL ISL
Point to temple then extend hand outward
❓
Question
Ask a question or seek information
ASL BSL ISL
Eyebrows raised, hand tilted forward
